Happy Independence Day

Seventy-nine years on from 1947, India marks its 80th Independence Day.

The freedom won that morning rests today on quieter things — institutions that hold, records that are honest, and rules applied the same way to everyone. Much of that work is unglamorous and goes unnoticed, which is rather the point.

Warm wishes to our clients, colleagues and friends, and to everyone who keeps those foundations in good repair.

Jai Hind.CA Vishal Bhandari · SVRJ & Co LLP · Hyderabad
