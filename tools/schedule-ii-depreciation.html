How this tool computes depreciation (Schedule II, Companies Act 2013)
  • SLM: annual charge = (Cost − Residual) ÷ Useful life, charged pro-rata on a day basis from the date the asset is put to use (Note 2 to Schedule II).
  • WDV: rate r = 1 − (Residual ÷ Cost)^(1 ÷ Life); charge = Opening WDV × r, pro-rated on days. Depreciation is capped so WDV never falls below residual value.
  • Residual value is capped at 5% of cost unless justified (Para 3, Part A). A higher figure is flagged.
  • Useful life for "other companies" must not exceed Part C; a longer life is flagged. A shorter life is permitted with disclosure & technical justification.
  • Extra shift (Note 6): for eligible (non-NESD) plant & machinery, depreciation on double-shift days is +50% and triple-shift days +100%. NESD items get no extra-shift loading.
  • Disposals are depreciated up to the disposal date; profit/(loss) on sale = Sale value − closing WDV.
  • Transition (Note 7): where remaining life is nil, the carrying amount (net of residual) is flagged for write-off against retained earnings.

All computation runs entirely in your browser — no asset data leaves this device. Useful lives are as per Schedule II, Companies Act 2013 (incl. amendments to 2016).
