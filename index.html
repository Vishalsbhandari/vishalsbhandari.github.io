Vishal Bhandari

Chartered Accountant · Manager, Audit and Advisory

A Chartered Accountant based in Hyderabad, working in audit and advisory. He is Manager, Audit and Advisory at SVRJ & Co LLP, where his work spans statutory, tax and internal audit, direct and indirect taxation, transfer pricing, and compliance for foreign-owned Indian subsidiaries.

Day to day that covers income tax and GST compliance and advisory, audit planning and execution, transfer pricing documentation and reporting, FEMA and foreign subsidiary filings, and virtual CFO support spanning monthly closing, MIS and financial statements. A substantial part of the work is litigation support — drafting replies to notices, appeals and written submissions, and assisting in representation before assessing officers and appellate authorities in income tax and GST matters.

He has worked with PwC Acceleration Centers, largely on US-based audit engagements, and with JSS Pro and R C Maheshwari & Associates, having completed his articleship with Phafat & Rathi, Chartered Accountants, in 2018. That combination of large-firm audit discipline and hands-on compliance work shapes how he approaches a file: scoped before it starts, supported by working papers, and documented so that positions hold up if a matter is later picked up in assessment or appeal.

Alongside his engagements he conducts in-house programmes for finance teams and sessions for professionals on taxation and audit topics, and builds the compliance tools published under Resources — calculators, reconciliation utilities and reference tools he uses in his own work and shares freely. He holds the ICAI Certificate Course on Artificial Intelligence for Chartered Accountants.

Notes on statutory change are posted under Articles and on LinkedIn.
