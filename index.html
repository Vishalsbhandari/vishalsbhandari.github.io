Services

What the practice handles

Engagements range from recurring compliance to one-off advisory and representation. Each area is handled directly, with a small team supporting execution.

Direct Taxation

Return preparation and review, computations across all heads of income, TDS and TCS compliance under the new payment-code framework, advance tax planning, and assessment support for companies, firms, trusts and individuals.

Income-tax Act, 2025 · Tax Year 2026-27

International Taxation

Treaty analysis and relief, residency and permanent establishment questions, withholding on cross-border payments, remittance certification, and foreign tax credit claims.

Form 145 / 146 — earlier 15CA / 15CB

Transfer Pricing

Benchmarking studies, method selection and comparability analysis, contemporaneous documentation, the accountant's report on international transactions, and support during transfer pricing assessments.

Form 48 — earlier 3CEB

Audit & Assurance

Statutory audit, tax audit on the consolidated single-form basis, internal and management audit, stock and revenue audits, and limited reviews — planned around materiality, controls testing and clean documentation.

Form 26 — earlier 3CA / 3CB / 3CD

GST

Registration, monthly and annual returns, input tax credit reconciliation, place-of-supply and classification opinions, refunds, departmental audits and notice replies.

CGST / SGST / IGST Acts, 2017

Foreign Subsidiary Compliance

Entity setup and ongoing compliance for foreign-owned Indian subsidiaries, inbound investment reporting, annual return filings, and reconciliation of parent and Indian reporting periods.

Virtual CFO Advisory

Practice

How engagements run

Work is scoped in writing before it starts, so the deliverable, the timeline and the fee basis are settled up front. Recurring compliance runs to a fixed calendar with reminders ahead of each due date; advisory and litigation matters are handled as they arise.

Documentation matters as much as the conclusion. Positions taken are supported by working papers and, where relevant, by statute and case law — which keeps files defensible if a matter is later picked up in assessment or appeal.

The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026, renumbering almost every section and every prescribed form. Two rulebooks therefore run side by side: current compliance follows the 2025 Act and the Income-tax Rules, 2026, while open assessments and appeals for earlier periods stay under the old law. Engagements are handled with that split in mind.

Where it saves time, compliance is supported by purpose-built tools for computation, reconciliation and reporting, rather than manual spreadsheets rebuilt each cycle.
