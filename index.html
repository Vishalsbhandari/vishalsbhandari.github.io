About

Vishal Bhandari

Chartered Accountant · Manager, Audit and Advisory

I am a Chartered Accountant based in Hyderabad and work as Manager, Audit and Advisory at SVRJ & Co LLP. My work runs across statutory, tax and internal audit, income tax and GST, transfer pricing, and compliance for foreign-owned Indian subsidiaries.

A good part of my time goes to international taxation and cross-border advisory — treaty positions and withholding on foreign remittances, transfer pricing documentation, FEMA filings, and advising clients on cash flow management, monthly closing and MIS. Before SVRJ I worked with PwC Acceleration Centers, largely on US audit engagements, and with JSS Pro and R C Maheshwari & Associates.

I also take sessions for finance teams and professionals, and build the tools published under Resources — calculators and reference utilities I use in my own work and share freely. What I write on statutory change goes under Articles and on LinkedIn.
